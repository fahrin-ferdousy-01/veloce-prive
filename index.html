Museums use the phrase for a loan that’s covered from the moment a work leaves its wall until it’s back on it. Ours work the same way.

What the fee covers

Delivery and collection, fully comprehensive insurance, a full tank, and 200 kilometres a day (150 for the Senna and the Chiron). Extra kilometres are charged at the rate on each car’s label.

The deposit

Held on a credit card in your name and released within seven days of collection. It’s also your excess, so it’s the most you could ever owe us.

Who can borrow

Anyone over 25 with a full licence held for three years. For the Senna and the Chiron, 30 and five years. Bring your passport, and an international permit outside the EU.

The courier

The person who looks after the car brings it to your home, hotel, marina or private terminal, between 7:00 and 23:00. Two days to move a car within Europe, ten to Dubai or Miami.

The condition report

You walk round the car together and both sign one page. It’s read again when the car is collected. Allow half an hour, longer if you’d like a first drive with them beside you.

Changing your mind

Free until 72 hours before delivery. After that, half the fee. If we ever have to cancel, you pay nothing and we offer you the next car in the collection.

List of works

All fees in euros, taxes included. Every car can be delivered in all five cities.
